## Quality and temporal correctness

Derived fields are versioned data products: model drift, prompt changes and label uncertainty
can alter their meaning. Maintain source lineage and independently reviewed samples; report
class-specific performance rather than hiding minority failures in one aggregate score.

Distributed delivery introduces duplication and reordering. Distinguish delivery guarantees
from end-to-end effect guarantees: even if a stream component suppresses duplicates, a downstream
proposal or write needs its own idempotency boundary. Define event-time windows, freshness limits
and correction policy explicitly.

## Production and research limits

Hold inputs fixed and test adverse timing distributions, outages and replay across restarts.
Report context age and refusal behavior alongside throughput. Small synthetic sequences do not
establish production scale or late-event distributions. A successful triage answer does not
authorize a transaction; retain authenticated approval, scoped execution and post-write verification.
