## Two outcomes, different architectures

Batch enrichment derives a reusable field and stores it for later analysis. Event triage
interprets a timely operational signal and proposes a response. Neither requires an unbounded
conversation, and a deterministic rule may solve the task more reliably than a model.

For enrichment, ask who owns the derived meaning and acceptable error rate. For event context,
ask how old the information can be and what happens when events arrive late or twice.
Fabric runs and stores governed data work; Foundry can provide model reasoning where needed.

## Evidence before action

Compare enrichment with independently labeled synthetic examples. Compare event processing
with expected outcomes under replay and out-of-order arrival. These lesson experiments are
designs, not executed live pipelines. An urgent signal still does not authorize an agent to
change records or perform a high-impact action.
