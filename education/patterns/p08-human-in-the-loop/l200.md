## Components and boundaries

| Component | Responsibility | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| Agent (Foundry, Copilot, Claude Code) | Gather context, draft the plan | Approve or execute |
| Change service | Plan, validate, store, enforce approval rules | Trust the agent's claims |
| Policy (`config/policies/writes.yaml`) | Operations, risk, rollback, destinations | Be bypassed by an overlay |
| Customer overlay | Narrow allowed writes and workspace aliases | Remove the approval step |
| Approver (human) | Accept the risk | Approve their own request |
| Scoped writer | Perform the write on the bound target | Hold broad permissions |
| Audit store | Append redacted records | Store tokens or secrets |

## Identity paths

- The **requester** identity is recorded on the plan.
- The **approver** identity must differ (separation of duties).
- The **writer** identity is separate again and least-privileged.

## Trade-offs

- More steps than a direct tool call — that friction is intentional for high-impact changes.
- Low-risk, reversible reads do not need this flow; the pattern applies to writes.

## Online and offline

| Destination | OFFLINE / LOCAL | LIVE |
|---|---|---|
| Execution | Simulated workspace, labeled `SIMULATED` | Needs `FFIA_ALLOW_LIVE_MUTATION=1`, approval and an authorized writer |
| If the writer is unavailable | — | `UNAVAILABLE`; **never** redirected to LOCAL |
