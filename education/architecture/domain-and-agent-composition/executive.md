## Boundaries before more agents

Two departments do not automatically need two agents. A domain describes who owns data and
what its terms mean. An agent boundary is justified when tools, permissions, context or release
ownership must be managed independently. More agents add coordination cost and failure modes.

Compare a deterministic router, one agent with bounded tools and separate domain agents.
Choose the smallest arrangement that meets the business and access requirements. Fabric IQ
multi-domain integration is an optional PREVIEW path, not a prerequisite for this decision.

## Evidence and accountability

Ask for correct routing, denied cross-domain access, supported answers and measured overhead.
The proposed synthetic study is not a live Fabric IQ demonstration. A coordinator may select
permitted context; it cannot grant access or approve a transactional write.
