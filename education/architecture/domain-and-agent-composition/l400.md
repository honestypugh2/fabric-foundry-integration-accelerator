## Systems and research review

Decomposition trades bounded context and independent ownership against coordination overhead,
partial failures and inconsistent semantics. An extra agent can reduce one prompt's complexity
while increasing total tool use and introducing another trust boundary. Neither domain count
nor a polished orchestration diagram establishes that the trade is beneficial.

Stratify evaluation by ambiguity, denied access and cross-domain reconciliation. Hold model,
identity, tool versions and expected decisions fixed. Report per-stratum errors and overhead,
not only aggregate answer accuracy. A deterministic local router cannot establish language-model
routing performance.

## Production acceptance

Require per-domain owners, authorization propagation, bounded fan-out, semantic conflict rules,
timeouts and durable audit. Validate preview availability and permissions separately. A passing
synthetic study does not certify Fabric IQ, end-user delegation or production multi-agent access.
