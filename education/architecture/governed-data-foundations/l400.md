## Theory meets systems constraints

Logical data independence does not mean all physical designs have equal cost. Partitioning,
file size, query planning, incremental updates and cache behavior affect performance while the
semantic contract should remain stable. Separate correctness experiments from tuning experiments.

## Research design

Use a fixed dataset and workload; vary one physical strategy at a time. Measure elapsed time,
scan volume, compute cost and failure behavior alongside exact output equivalence. Repeat runs
and disclose warm-cache effects rather than presenting a single favorable timing.

## Limits and threats to validity

Synthetic distributions may not reflect skew, concurrency or scale. A row-count match can hide
different values. Correct totals can hide unauthorized disclosure. Add content-level comparison
and permission-negative tests before claiming production readiness.

## Production handoff

Document business ownership, lineage, schema evolution, rollback and entitlement checks.
Keep live optimization separately approved; this lesson does not authorize changing a capacity,
workspace, semantic model or pipeline.
