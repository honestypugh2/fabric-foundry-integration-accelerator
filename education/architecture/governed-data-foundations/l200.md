## From logical design to implementation

Specify grain, candidate keys, foreign keys, units and allowed nulls before selecting storage.
Then express the transformation as relational operations: selection filters rows, projection
chooses columns and a join combines compatible facts through an explicit relationship.

## When and when not

Use medallion separation when provenance, repeatable correction and multiple consumers matter.
A tiny one-off analysis may not justify a multi-layer platform. Do not treat Gold as a promise
that every anomaly has vanished; some quality flags should remain visible.

## Applied experiment

Use a copy of synthetic inputs. Compare a query with unique dimension keys against the same
query after injecting one duplicate dimension row. Hold facts, filters and arithmetic fixed.
Measure revenue inflation and the number of failed key checks.

## Expected result

A duplicate-sensitive join should fail a contract check or change the observed aggregate.
Recover by fixing the key contract upstream, then rebuild and reconcile. Do not patch the
final report to hide inconsistent data.
