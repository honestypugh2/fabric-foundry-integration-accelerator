## Why meaning comes before intelligence

A confident answer over inconsistent data is still wrong. Before buying a more capable model,
agree what the business counts, which records belong in a measure, and who may see them.
Fabric can provide this governed context; an agent does not repair an undefined metric.

## The ideas behind today's tools

Relational thinking separates the meaning of data from its storage. Warehousing adds reusable
business definitions. Lakehouse layering organizes raw, validated and serving data. A semantic
model exposes named measures so reports and agents need not independently reinvent arithmetic.
These are conceptual connections, not a claim that one product directly implements every paper.

## Decision and evidence

Choose a synthetic measure, name its owner, define its population and compare the report and
agent with the same baseline. Success means reconciled values and permitted access, not merely
a visually convincing dashboard. Ask which failures remain undetected and who owns recovery.
