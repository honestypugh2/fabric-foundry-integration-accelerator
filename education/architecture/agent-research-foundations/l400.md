## Threats to validity

Prompt sensitivity, evaluator bias, small samples, model changes and task leakage can dominate
apparent improvements. Report the sample size, failures, variance and excluded runs. A single
successful response is a case study, not an estimate of reliability.

## Measure the system, not one score

Evaluate retrieval relevance, tool selection, argument validity, grounded claims, numeric
correctness, latency, cost and unauthorized-action refusal separately. Add permission-negative
and outage cases. A LOCAL fallback is legitimate service continuity but cannot certify a
LIVE agent integration.

## Causal interpretation

An ablation suggests a component's contribution only under its controlled conditions. Results
from synthetic sales data do not establish performance on customer data or in another industry.
The original research papers provide hypotheses and mechanisms, not product guarantees.

## Production boundary

Use versioned evaluation datasets and review regression thresholds before deployment.
Revalidate identity, data access and approval enforcement independently of answer quality.
Keep preview integrations optional and clearly labeled.
