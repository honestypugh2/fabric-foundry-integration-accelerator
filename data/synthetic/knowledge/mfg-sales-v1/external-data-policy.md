# External and public data use policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer policy written for education. Not real guidance.

## Allowed sources

Public market data may be used from approved providers through a grounding service with logged
queries. Collecting data from a competitor's website requires a legal review of the site's terms
of use before any automated access.

## Handling

External content is treated as untrusted input. It is never mixed into governed sales tables, is
cited with its source and retrieval date, and is screened for prompt injection before an agent
uses it.

## Prohibited

Automated scraping that bypasses access controls, collection of personal data, and sending
internal data to external services without approval.
