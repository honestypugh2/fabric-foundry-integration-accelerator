# Booked revenue definition

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer policy written for education. Not real guidance.

## What counts as booked revenue

Booked revenue is the sum of quantity times unit price for order lines in the month they were
ordered. Order lines with status Cancelled are excluded. Lines flagged by a data-quality rule are
excluded until a data steward resolves them.

## Returns and credits

Returns reduce revenue in the month the credit note is issued, not in the month of the original
order. Credits are recorded in the finance system, which remains the authority for revenue.

## Currency

All sales are booked in US dollars. No currency conversion is applied in the sales model.
