# Monthly business-team brief policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer policy written for education. Not real guidance.

## Audience and timing

Each business team receives one brief in the first five business days of the month. The brief
covers the previous month for the team's focus area only.

## Content

A brief states booked revenue, change versus the previous month and the same month last year,
target attainment, gross margin and the top growing product. Every number must come from the
governed sales model, never from free text.

## Approval before sending

A sales operations lead approves each brief before it is sent. Automated drafting is allowed;
automated sending is not. Sent briefs are recorded in the audit log.
